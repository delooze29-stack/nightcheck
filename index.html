How it works

  1. Load the exports, then print the sheet. There's one numbered row per lift.
  2. On the floor, write S or E + height for standard/euro pallets (e.g. S70, E80), or full L, W, H for oversize. Only write kg if it's different to booked.
  3. On step 3, type the figures in (S70 / E80, or full L W H for oversize). Press Enter to jump to the next box, or the down arrow to go straight to the same box on the next lift. Type = in S / E to copy the lift above, or press Same as lift 1 on a multi-lift con. Leave kg blank to use the booked weight.
  4. As each con is filled in, it shows straight away what to change on Palletways and what to charge.

Charging rule: re-key each pallet to what it should be. If the new revenue is higher, leave it. If it's lower, set it back to the original. Oversize and overweight pallets are priced on the SK rate (that pallet's rate card × %).

Assumptions – check these:
  • Pallet type rules follow the UK Pallet Spec 2026, the same as the Pallet Keyer.
  • SK oversize %: the longest side is the "front-facing" width/length, and the shorter side is the depth.
  • SK overweight %: up to 1200kg = 100%, 1201–1300 = 135%, 1301–1400 = 170%, 1401–1500 = 195%, over 1500 = POA.
  • If a pallet is both oversize and overweight, the extra percentages are added together. For example, 175% and 135% makes 210%.
  • Oversize price: revenue − ticked extras (tail lift £3, handball £5, AM £20, timed £25, Saturday £45), × the SK %, then the extras go back on. Timed and Saturday are pre-ticked from the service – check them.
